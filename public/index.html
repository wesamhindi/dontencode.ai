          GOT QUESTIONS?
          
Frequently Asked Questions

          
Plain-English answers about how DoNotEncode.ai v0.6 protects your website and gets you recommended by AI.

        


        

          

            
              Why is the platform called DoNotEncode.ai?
              
            
            

              Because "encoding" or scrambling your website code is a fake lock. Today's AI tools can unscramble encoded code in less than one second. Instead of scrambling your code, DoNotEncode.ai locks it inside a private digital vault that works normally for real shoppers, but looks completely blank to copycats.
            

          


          

            
              How can your system tell when an AI agent visits my page or recommends my product?
              
            
            

              Every time a human asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, or Google AI for a product recommendation, that AI assistant sends a special live lookup request to your website with its own official digital ID badge. Our edge sensor spots that badge instantly, logs which product page the AI read, and also tracks every time a customer clicks your link inside an AI chat window to buy from you.
            

          


          

            
              Will locking my website code hurt my Google SEO or AI Search rankings?
              
            
            

              No—it actually boosts your rankings! While we lock out copycats and scraper bots, we roll out the red carpet for verified search engines and AI assistants. We hand ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google a super-clean, always-updated summary of your products and prices so they recommend you #1.
            

          


          

            
              How does File & Cloud Lock protect my images, videos, and private downloads?
              
            
            

              Normally, anyone can right-click "Inspect" on your website, grab the direct links to your cloud files or videos, and download everything in bulk. Our File Lock puts a one-time visitor pass on every file link that expires the split-second your webpage finishes loading.
            

          


          

            
              Do I need to be a programmer to install DoNotEncode.ai v0.6?
              
            
            

              Not at all. Setup takes under 10 seconds with a single copy-paste command. It automatically detects your website platform (Shopify, Next.js, WordPress, Vercel, or Cloudflare) and turns on all 6 protection layers plus Automatic AI SEO right away.
