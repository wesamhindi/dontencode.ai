          GOT QUESTIONS?
          
Frequently Asked Questions

          
Everything you need to know about how DoNotEncode.ai protects your web app without hurting SEO or speed.

        


        

          

            
              Why is the platform called DoNotEncode.ai?
              
            
            

              Because encoding (like Base64, hex strings, or basic JavaScript obfuscation) is a false sense of security—modern AI coding agents and browser DevTools reverse-engineer encoded bundles in milliseconds. Instead of encoding, DoNotEncode.ai compiles sensitive logic into polymorphic stack bytecode (_aVM) and enforces cryptographic single-use edge tickets.
            

          


          

            
              Will protecting my JavaScript hurt my Google SEO or AI Search rankings?
              
            
            

              No—it actually improves your rankings. DoNotEncode.ai never blocks legitimate search crawlers or breaks Server-Side Rendering (SSR). Even better, our Living AI Search Engine automatically generates and signs a clean /llms.txt manifest from your live pages so ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI cite your product first without wading through 90% HTML/JS noise.
            

          


          

            
              What does the Live URL Scanner check when I enter my website?
              
            
            

              Our scanner performs a real-time non-destructive HTTP/TLS inspection of your URL: it checks for missing browser security headers (CSP, HSTS, COOP), inspects public <script> bundles for exposed API routes and cloud keys (AWS S3, Supabase, Firebase), checks for leaked Next.js/Nuxt/Shopify hydration state, and verifies whether /llms.txt is present for AI search engines.
            

          


          

            
              How does Cloud Vault protect AWS S3, Supabase, and Firebase URLs?
              
            
            

              Normally, anyone who opens the browser Network tab can copy your direct S3 bucket URLs, AI model weights, or Supabase anon keys and replay them in a scraper script. Cloud Vault rewrites those URLs at the edge into single-use, session-bound HMAC tickets that expire immediately after the browser loads the asset.
            

          


          

            
              How fast is installation on Next.js, Shopify, or Cloudflare/Vercel?
              
            
            

              Under 10 seconds. Run npx donotencode init --target https://yoursite.com (or --dir ./your-repo) and the CLI automatically generates a zero-dependency Edge Worker and /llms.txt manifest ready to deploy on Cloudflare Workers, Vercel Edge, or Node.js.
